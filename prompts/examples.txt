## Example

This example shows the format only. Example Corp and its figures are invented: never use them as facts.

Question: What share of revenue did Example Corp spend on research and development in fiscal 2024?

Example Corp spent $$1,200 million on research and development in fiscal 2024 [D41], against total revenue of $$10,000 million [D38]. That is 12.0% of revenue [C5e0b7a].

### Assumptions
- "Spend on research and development" means the research and development operating expense line.
